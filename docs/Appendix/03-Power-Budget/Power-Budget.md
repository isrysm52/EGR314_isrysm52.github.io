## Overview
This power budget was created to make sure that the wall power supply could supply the esp32. This is for a greander scheme of a whole system even though this subsystem does not use a whole lot of current. 

![budget1](Power_Budget_A1.png){style width:"350" height:"300;"}

## Conclusions

From the prepare Power Budget, this subsystem has plenty of power from a 9V power supply. The only two main components are the esp32 and the OLED screen which means they come out to a little more than 500mA.
