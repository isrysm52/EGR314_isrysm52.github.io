## Introduction

* **This is subsystem A1 of the duck. The subsystem constitutes the OLED screen and buttons used to communicate direction and controll of multiple subsystems in the boat we have created.**

### Project Summary

* This subsystem will be connected to A2, a wireless subsystem, that connecte to the whole daisy chain in the boat. Both "A" subsystems are the whole controller that we use. This will be outside the boat and connected to a wall jack for power. We anticipated that this would be on a bigger boat, like a cruise ship, and the smaller boat would go out and survey the outer hull of the boat and the surrounding area. 
* For morte information about other subsystems and the whole team project here is the [team report.](https://egr314-s-2026-201.github.io/).
